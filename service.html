ABOUT US

Welcome to our Organ Donation Service. We are dedicated to saving lives through organ and tissue donation. Our mission is to provide comprehensive support and information to donors and recipients.

OUR SERVICES

We offer the following services:

  • Organ donation registration
  • Organ recipient registration
  • Information and support for donors and recipients
  • Coordination of organ transplants
  • Education and awareness programs
